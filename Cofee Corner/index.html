How your coffee is made

Four unhurried steps between a green seed and the cup on your counter.

1

Source

We taste dozens of samples each season and buy only the lots we'd happily drink every day.

2

Roast

Each origin gets its own profile, logged curve by curve so the flavour lands the same way twice.

3

Cup

Every batch is cupped on a slurp spoon before it's cleared to bag — quality control you can taste.

4

Brew

Across the counter or at your kitchen bench, we send brew guides so it tastes as intended.
