Roast — Free Bootstrap 5 Coffee Shop & Roastery Template by uiCookies
https://uicookies.com/

Roast is a free, warm-hearted website template for specialty coffee shops, roasteries, and neighbourhood cafés. It ships two pages — a one-page home and a full café menu with a filterable single-origin bean shop — and includes a working shopping bag, roast-level filtering, an accessible photo lightbox, count-up stats, and scroll animations. Everything is built on Bootstrap 5 with plain vanilla JavaScript: no jQuery and no plugin dependencies. Content stays fully visible without JavaScript (progressive enhancement) and the interactions respect reduced-motion preferences. Free for personal and commercial use — swap the demo copy, prices, hours, and placeholder images for your own before launch. See https://uicookies.com/license/ for terms.
